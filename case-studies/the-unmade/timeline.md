## Historical reconstruction method

Original GDD/TDD/spec status lines must be read alongside **later dated execution reports**. A spec initially labelled Planned may be superseded by source or Editor implementation, without meeting Tested or Human Validated. The [feature inventory](features/README.md) records what is supported by the reviewed evidence, not a guessed final status.

The jam's historical listed delivery deadline was **Nov 16, 01:59 in mainland Portugal**. This timeline does not assert an actual uploaded entry because a verified submission receipt was not included in the reviewed report set.
