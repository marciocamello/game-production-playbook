## Interpreting evidence

**Spec published** ≠ **work implemented** ≠ **Editor asset integrated** ≠ **automated tests passed** ≠ **human PIE validated** ≠ **fresh packaged-game completion**.

For any feature being reused in another tutorial, trace its approval and code/asset revisions, individual test reports and known failures before claiming a final state. The source game is private: do not reproduce scripts, paths, screenshots or commits containing private data without a separate rights and sanitization review.
