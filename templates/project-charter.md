## Candidate evaluation

For each real concept considered, record **fun/clarity**, novelty, implementation scope, art/animation need, legal/technical risk and a reason for approval or rejection. Do not invent concepts that were never evaluated.

## Production priority

- **Must-have:** the smallest complete and shippable game.
- **Optional:** work that cannot block the required critical route.
- **Cut/excluded:** features deliberately excluded.
- **Risks:** dependencies, mitigation, and trigger for a scope cut.
